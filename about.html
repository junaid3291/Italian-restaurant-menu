THE CRAFT

Restraint is the highest form of culinary confidence.

At Osteria Aurelia, we strip away unnecessary ornamentation to let extraordinary Italian raw ingredients speak clearly. Each dish is composed of no more than four to five core elements sourced directly from small family estates in Emilia-Romagna, Campania, and Piedmont.

Our dining room is designed with warm limestone, natural linen, and soft candlelight so every guest feels calm from the first pour.
